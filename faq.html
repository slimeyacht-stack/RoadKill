FAQ

Most questions we get, answered. Haven't found yours? Email hi@roadkillbrand.com.

We're from northeastern Pennsylvania — grit, cold, and highway energy. The "side of the road" aesthetic comes straight from the turnpikes and truck stops of PA.

Our whole brand is "damaged goods." We intentionally leave wear, bites, tears and scuffs on every piece. It's the design, not a defect.

Grab a spot on the list on our home page or the Drop List section. We'll send early access to the first 200 before the drop goes live.

Orders ship within 1–2 business days via USPS Priority. Expect 3–5 business days on the East Coast, 5–8 elsewhere.

All intentional damage is pre-approved. If something arrives with unexpected damage, reply to your order confirmation and we'll make it right — no questions, no returns hassle.

Shipping is free on orders over $100. Under $100, standard USPS Priority is $7.99. We'll show the exact price at checkout.

Due to the nature of the brand, we don't accept returns on unworn items. However, if a piece arrives with unexpected damage, we'll make it right — and the intentional damage is pre-approved.
