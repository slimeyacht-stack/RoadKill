About ROADKILL

Built for the bad winter.

ROADKILL is a declaration: damage isn't a flaw, it's a badge. The right pair of jeans doesn't just fit — it tells a story. The right beanie doesn't just keep you warm, it says you've been through something.

We're from northeastern Pennsylvania — grit, cold, and highway energy. The "side of the road" aesthetic comes straight from the turnpikes and truck stops of PA. We make gear that looks like it survived it.

Every piece is cut and finished by hand. Every stain is real. The damage you see was deliberate, and the gear you wear is the point.

Our flagship is the Ripped Long-Ear Bunny Beanie: a wide, snug cold-weather beanie with one ear deliberately chewed to a rounded point, like a dog got to it in the back of a hot car. It's the detail that makes you smile, every time you put it on.

This is not a flaw. It's the design.

The Damage Manifesto

We don't fix what isn't broken. We don't polish, we don't press, we don't hide the wear. The scuffs are the story. The fray is the detail. The bite is the signature.

Wear it like you lived it. Order it like you're already on the list.
