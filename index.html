FAQ

We're from northeastern Pennsylvania — grit, cold, and highway energy. The "side of the road" aesthetic comes straight from the turnpikes and truck stops of PA.

Our whole brand is "damaged goods." We intentionally leave wear, bites, tears and scuffs on every piece. It's the design, not a defect.
